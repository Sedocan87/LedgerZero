# Video Tutorial Script: Migrating from YNAB

**Duration**: about 2 minutes
**Format**: phone screen recording with voiceover
Reference: [YNAB_MIGRATION.md](../YNAB_MIGRATION.md)

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
**Visual**: LedgerZero Home screen.
**Audio**: "This video shows how to move a YNAB budget into LedgerZero:
accounts, categories, transactions and budgeted amounts."

## Scene 2: Export from YNAB (0:15 - 0:40)
**Visual**: YNAB web app, budget menu, export; the downloaded ZIP is
extracted.
**Audio**: "In the YNAB web app, export your budget. You get a ZIP file with
two CSV files: Register and Budget. Unzip it and save both files where your
phone can open them, for example in Google Drive or Downloads."

## Scene 3: Start the migration (0:40 - 1:10)
**Action**:
1. Tap the gear icon on Home to open Settings.
2. Under **Manage data**, tap **YNAB Migration**.
3. Choose **nYNAB (CSV Export)**.
4. Select the Register file, then the Budget file.
5. Tap **Next: Preview**.
**Audio**: "Open Settings, then YNAB Migration under Manage data. Choose
nYNAB, select the Register and the Budget file, and tap Next."

## Scene 4: Preview and import (1:10 - 1:40)
**Visual**: Import Preview with the Accounts and Categories tabs and the
counts of transactions and budget entries.
**Action**: Scroll the lists, tap **Confirm & Import**, show the progress
screen.
**Audio**: "The preview lists the accounts and categories that will be
created and how many transactions and budget entries were found. Tap Confirm
& Import and keep the app open until it finishes."

## Scene 5: Result (1:40 - 2:00)
**Visual**: Success screen, tap **Start Budgeting**, Budget tab with data.
**Audio**: "Done. Your history is now in LedgerZero. Check each account's
type and currency: imported accounts start as US dollars, and you can change
that in the account settings."

**Production notes**: start from a fresh install (importing twice duplicates
transactions). YNAB 4 users choose **YNAB 4 (Classic)** and select the
`.ynab4` folder instead of the two files.
