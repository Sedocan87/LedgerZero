# Video Tutorial Script: YNAB to LedgerZero Migration

**Title**: Switching to Freedom: Migrating from YNAB to LedgerZero
**Duration**: ~3 minutes

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visual**: Host speaking to camera or LedgerZero logo with animated text.
**Audio**: "Are you tired of monthly subscriptions for your budget? Welcome to LedgerZero. In this video, we'll show you how to migrate your entire financial history from YNAB to LedgerZero in just a few minutes, so you can own your data forever."

## Scene 2: Preparation (0:30 - 1:00)
**Visual**: Screen recording of YNAB Web Interface.
**Action**:
1. Click on "My Budget" (top left).
2. Click "Export Budget".
3. Show the downloaded zip file being extracted.
**Audio**: "First, head over to YNAB. Click on your budget name and select 'Export Budget'. This will give you a zip file. Extract it to find 'Register.csv' and 'Budget.csv'. That's all you need."

## Scene 3: The Migration Wizard (1:00 - 2:00)
**Visual**: Screen recording of LedgerZero App (Mobile or Desktop).
**Action**:
1. Open LedgerZero.
2. Tap Settings -> Import Data -> YNAB Migration.
3. Select "nYNAB (CSV Export)".
4. Tap "Select Register.csv" -> Pick file.
5. Tap "Select Budget.csv" -> Pick file.
**Audio**: "Now, open LedgerZero. Go to Settings, Import Data, and choose YNAB Migration. Select 'nYNAB'. You'll be asked for the two files we just downloaded. Pick 'Register.csv' for your transactions and 'Budget.csv' for your budget history."

## Scene 4: Preview & Import (2:00 - 2:30)
**Visual**: App showing the Preview screen. Scrolling through accounts list.
**Action**:
1. Scroll through the preview list.
2. Tap "Confirm & Import".
3. Show the progress spinner/bar.
**Audio**: "LedgerZero analyzes your files and shows you a preview. You can see all your accounts and categories ready to be created. Looks good? Hit 'Confirm & Import'. Sit back while we process your history."

## Scene 5: Success & Result (2:30 - 3:00)
**Visual**: Success screen appears. Tap "Start Budgeting". Show the Budget screen populated with data.
**Audio**: "And you're done! All your transactions, categories, and budget allocations are now in LedgerZero. No subscriptions, 100% private. Happy budgeting!"

## Scene 6: Outro (3:00+)
**Visual**: LedgerZero Logo and download link.
**Audio**: "Download LedgerZero today and take back control of your money."
