# Mint to LedgerZero: Key Differences

Moving from Mint to LedgerZero involves a shift in mindset from "Expense Tracking" to "Zero-Based Budgeting".

## Philosophy

| Feature | Mint | LedgerZero |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Methodology** | **Expense Tracking**: focus on where money went. | **Zero-Based Budgeting**: focus on giving every dollar a job before it's spent. |
| **Data Privacy** | **Cloud-Based**: Your data is on Intuit's servers. | **Local-First**: Your data stays on your device (encrypted). |
| **Business Model** | **Ad-Supported**: Free, but you are the product. | **One-Time Purchase**: You pay for the software, no ads/subs. |

## Terminology

| Mint Term | LedgerZero Term | Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Budget** | **Category Budget** / **Envelope** | In Mint, budgets are often loose goals. In LedgerZero, they are hard limits based on available funds. |
| **Tag** | **Tag** (in Memo) | LedgerZero stores tags within the transaction memo/notes (e.g., `#vacation`). |
| **Account** | **Account** | Same concept. LedgerZero supports Checking, Savings, Credit, Cash. |
| **Sub-Category** | **Category** | LedgerZero has Category Groups (Folders) and Categories. Mint's sub-categories map to Categories. |
| **Everything Else** | **To Be Budgeted (TBB)** | Unassigned money sits in TBB until you give it a job. |

## Feature Mapping

*   **Trends**: LedgerZero has a **Reports** tab with Spending by Category, Payee, and Net Worth.
*   **Bills**: LedgerZero uses **Scheduled Transactions** for recurring bills.
*   **Goals**: LedgerZero allows setting **Target Balances** and **Monthly Funding Goals** on categories.

## Transition Tips

*   **Don't obsess over history**: Importing 10 years of Mint history is great for trends, but for *budgeting*, only the present matters. Focus on getting your current balances correct.
*   **Simplify Categories**: Mint often has hundreds of auto-generated categories. Take this opportunity to consolidate them into a simpler structure that reflects your actual spending habits.
*   **Reconcile Often**: Unlike Mint (which auto-syncs), you are in control. Reconciling your accounts regularly ensures your budget is accurate.
