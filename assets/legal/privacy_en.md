# Privacy policy

Effective: {{effectiveDate}}

LedgerZero is a budgeting app that keeps your data on your phone. There is no LedgerZero account, no LedgerZero server, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. This policy explains which data the app handles, where it goes, and your rights.

## Who is responsible

{{publisherBlock}}

## Data on your phone

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, budgets, categories, payees, notes, rules and settings) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We, the publisher, never receive it.

- **App lock.** If you turn on the app lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app only learns whether the check succeeded; it never sees biometric data.
- **Error reports.** If you turn on crash reports (Settings > Security), error details are kept on your phone. They are only sent if you choose to send them to us yourself, for example by email.

## Optional features

- **Card notifications.** If you give LedgerZero access to notifications, the app reads notifications of the banking and wallet apps you choose, to book card payments. This happens on your phone; the notification texts are not sent anywhere.
- **Wi-Fi sync.** If you pair phones, they exchange your budget directly over your local Wi-Fi network, end-to-end encrypted with your sync passphrase. No server is involved, and nobody without the passphrase can read the data.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code for Wi-Fi sync. The image is processed on your phone and not stored.
- **Backups.** Backup files are encrypted with your backup password and saved wherever you choose (for example a folder, a USB stick or a cloud app of your choice). The provider you choose processes the file under its own terms.
- **Android backup.** If Android backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android stores a copy of the app's data in your Google account, end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. Google processes this backup under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Reminders.** Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your phone.

## Google Play

You download LedgerZero, buy LedgerZero Pro and rate the app through Google Play. Google processes the data needed for this (for example your Google account and payment details) as its own controller under the Google Privacy Policy.

- For purchases, Google gives us the order information we need for accounting, tax and refunds: order number, date, product, price, country and, depending on Google's reports, the buyer's name.
- Google also gives us statistics about installs, ratings and app crashes. Crash and performance data comes only from phones whose users allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics with Google; we see it without your name or contact details.
- Ratings and reviews you write are shown by Google; we see them in the Play Console.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), our legal obligations under tax law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in a working and improving app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Contacting us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete them when your request is settled, unless we have to keep them longer by law.

## How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app, reset the app or uninstall it. Order information is kept as long as tax law requires (in Germany up to ten years).

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Contact us at {{email}}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

Because your budget data never reaches us, we cannot show, change or delete it for you; you control it in the app (export, delete, factory reset).

## Children

LedgerZero is not directed at children under 16.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown in the app and on our website with a new effective date.
